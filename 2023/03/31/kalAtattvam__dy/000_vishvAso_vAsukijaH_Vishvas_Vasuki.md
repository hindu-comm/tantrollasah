    वित्-कला-नियतिषु** स्यात् ।
    प्रत्य्-एकं भुवन-द्वयम्

    iti vartate tantrasāre

  

tatra hemantāryaḥ -  
  
The principles of impure vidyā, kalā, and niyati have two worlds each, The names of the worlds in impure vidyā, kalā, and niyati are Krodheśa and Canda, Samvarta and Jyoti, Sūra and Pancānta, respectively.

  

iti

  

  

anyatra tu -  

  

    अष्टादश-भुवनिका **शान्ता**

    ityatra 

Every principle is composed of subtle elements (kalās). They are called nivr̥tti, pratiṣṭhā, vidyā, śāntā, and sāntātītā. The number of worlds in nivr̥ttikalā is sixteen; in pratiṣṭhā the number is fifty six; in vidyā the number is twenty-eight; in śāntā the number is eighteen. There are no worlds in śāntātītā. Thus, the total number of the worlds in all five kalās is one hundred eighteen.

  

iti hemantāryaḥ.

  

  

atroktayoḥ kalāśabdayoḥ ko bhedaḥ? adhas tv ekam eva tattvaṁ tannāmnā vartate
