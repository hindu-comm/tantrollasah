शिवाय नमः ॥

Though apparaently it looks incongruent to find the name Abhinavagupta in the body of the text, we do find an instance where Abhinavagupta mentions his name in the 3rd invocatory verse:

श्रीशम्भुनाथभास्करचरणनिपातप्रभापगतसंकोचम् ।

अभिनवगुप्तहृदम्बुजम् एतद् विचिनुत महेशपूजनहेतोः॥

  

Only at 2 places it seems Abhinavagupta uses the compound अस्मद्गुरवः to denote his Guru.  

On the contrary in the body of the Tantrasara we find 4 places where अभिनवगुप्तगुरवः is repeated.  

From this it becomes evident that Abhinavagupta had his own convention in directly mentioning himself by his name and not by such words as अस्माभिः, मया, etc. which we normally find in many other texts.  

So I do not find any *असमञ्जसम्* in these usages and they are all very much the words of Abhinavaguptacharya. Consequently there is no need to go in for any 'correction' or to search for any शुद्धपाठ .
