तन्त्रसारानुवादप्रस्तावे [https://archive.org/details/TantrasaraOfAbhinavaguptaH.N.Chakravarti/page/n11/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/TantrasaraOfAbhinavaguptaH.N.Chakravarti/page/n11/mode/2up) इत्यत्र -  
"According to this śavādvaya system, ignorance is accepted as the cause of saṁsāra, yet the nature of ajñāna is known as āṇava-mala, a limitation innate in the individual."

  

शिवाद्वयो वा शैवाद्वयो वा स्याद्वेति सन्देह्मि - तथापीमे तान्त्रिकाः तादृशान्य् अपि नामानि प्रयुञ्जीरन्न् इति नात्रापि विस्रम्भः।
