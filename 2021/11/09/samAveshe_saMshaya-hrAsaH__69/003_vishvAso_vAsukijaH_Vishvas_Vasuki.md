असंशीतिफलाय तत्त्वावेशलाभाय महान् प्रयासः स्वाभाविको भाति। हगाकुरेग्रन्थात् -  

  

The priest Tannen used to say,

> 'People come to no understanding because priests teach only the
> doctrine of’No Mind.’What is called’No Mind’is a mind that is pure
> and lacks complication.'

This is interesting.

Lord Sanenori said,“In the midst of a single breath,where perversity cannot be held,is the Way.” If so,then the Way is one.

But there is no one who can understand this clarity at first.Purity is something that cannot be attained except by piling effort upon effort.
